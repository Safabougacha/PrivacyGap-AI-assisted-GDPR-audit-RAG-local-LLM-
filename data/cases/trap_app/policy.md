# StepWell (fictional app) — privacy policy

> **Fictional case.** StepWell does not exist. It is a fitness and wellbeing app built
> as a **trap case**: some problems are subtle (vague wording, bundled consent,
> undisclosed transfer), and some flows look alarming but are actually compliant.
> A good audit tool must catch the first without over-reacting to the second.

**P1.** By creating an account you accept our Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy, and
you agree to receive personalised offers from us and our partners.

**P2.** We collect your steps, heart rate and sleep data to show you your activity and
wellbeing trends.

**P3.** We may share some data with selected partners to improve our services and offer
you relevant products.

**P4.** With your permission (pop-up when you first open the map), we share your
approximate location with our advertising partner to show you local gym offers. You
can turn this off at any time in Settings.

**P5.** We keep your data for as long as necessary.

**P6.** Our technical providers (hosting, crash reporting) process data on our behalf
under a contract.

**P7.** If you subscribe to our newsletter (unticked box), we send you tips by email.
