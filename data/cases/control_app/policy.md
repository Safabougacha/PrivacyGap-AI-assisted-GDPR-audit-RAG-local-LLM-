# ClearCycle (fictional app) — privacy policy

> **Fictional case.** ClearCycle does not exist. It is a period-tracking app designed
> to follow GDPR good practice, used as a **control case**: a reliable audit tool
> should raise few or no alerts here.

**P1.** Your cycle, symptom and pregnancy data are health data. We process them only
with your **explicit consent**, which you give on a separate screen and can withdraw
at any time in Settings.

**P2.** We use this data only to provide predictions and insights to you. We never use
it for advertising and we never sell it.

**P3.** We do not include any advertising SDK in the app.

**P4.** Our service providers (hosting in the EU, crash reporting, email delivery) act
as processors under a written contract (GDPR art. 28). They cannot use your data for
their own purposes.

**P5.** We measure app usage only with aggregated, anonymous statistics, with your
consent to analytics cookies and trackers.

**P6.** We keep your account data for as long as your account is active, and delete it
within 30 days after you close your account.

**P7.** All data is stored in the European Union. We do not transfer personal data
outside the EU.
