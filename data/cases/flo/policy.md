# Flo Health — privacy policy commitments (2016–2019), reconstructed

> **Reconstruction notice.** This is not the full original policy. It gathers the
> commitments Flo made to users, as quoted in the FTC complaint (2021), the
> class action *Frasco v. Flo Health* and press coverage, and as summarised in
> the case study "Flo App Scandal: Period Data Exposed" (RSB, November 2025).
> Each statement is numbered so that the audit can quote it.

**P1.** Your health data is kept private and secure.

**P2.** We use the information you enter only to provide the app's services: cycle
tracking, predictions and health insights.

**P3.** We will not share information regarding your marked cycles, pregnancy,
symptoms, notes and other information entered by you with third parties.

**P4.** We may share data with third parties only when this is needed for essential
technical services, such as web hosting and app development. Such sharing never
includes intimate health details.

**P5.** We may use technical identifiers and app usage information to understand how
the app is used.
